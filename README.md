## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings
* **Budget Optimization:** Overall actual project spending ($937,169.00) stayed well within the total planned budget ($1,279,500.00), achieving a budget saving of ~26.7%.
* **Departmental Bottlenecks:** The IT department recorded the highest number of delayed tasks (9 delayed tasks) and highest average delay days, followed by Marketing (8 delayed tasks) and Finance (7 delayed tasks).
* **Cost Allocation by Department:** Operations generated the highest actual spending for completed tasks ($103,532.00), whereas Quality Assurance had the lowest cost expenditure ($51,635.00).
* **Delay Variance by Priority:** Delayed tasks with "Medium Priority" experienced the highest average schedule slip (~11.58 days), whereas "High Priority" delayed tasks averaged a lower slip (~4.80 days).
